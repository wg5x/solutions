# Common FAQ

## What does the buyer get?

A clearly scoped deliverable, such as a report, cleaned table, draft document, structured knowledge package, or assisted analysis output.

## What must the buyer provide?

The topic, source materials, target audience, desired format, and any must-keep constraints.

## How long does delivery take?

The delivery time depends on the package tier and the completeness of the provided materials.

## How many revisions are included?

Each package must state a revision cap in its own `listing.md` and `pricing.md`.

## Is my data kept private?

Only the materials needed for delivery are used. Do not upload secrets or unrelated private data.

## What is not included?

Off-platform actions, legal guarantees, signature, payment, publishing, and any work not listed in the package scope are excluded.
