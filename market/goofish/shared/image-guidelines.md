# Image Guidelines

Each listing uses 4 PNG images:

1. Cover: product name, scenario, outcome
2. Deliverables: files or outputs the buyer receives
3. Workflow: input -> process -> review -> delivery
4. Inputs and boundaries: what the buyer must provide and what is not included

Rules:

- PNG size: 1080x1440
- No prices in any image
- No discount language in any image
- No phone numbers, email addresses, QR codes, URLs, or external contact words in any image
- No platform names for external contact or off-platform handoff
- No unverifiable promises
- Keep text readable on mobile
