# Publish Checklist

## Before publishing

- Product title is copied from `listing.md`
- Category is confirmed in the live publish page
- Price is copied from `pricing.md`
- Inventory / acceptance quantity is set consciously
- Description includes scope, delivery, inputs, exclusions, and revision count
- Four images are attached in the right order
- No image contains price, discount, contact, QR code, URL, or off-platform contact words
- No real customer data, secrets, or unauthorized materials are used
- The package status is acceptable for publishing

## After publishing

- Reopen the live listing once to confirm title, images, and price
- Verify no image text was compressed or cut off
- Keep the published page reference for manual review
